Follow these steps to set up the application on your computer:

Once the installation finishes, locate the Apex MMC icon on your desktop. Double-click the icon to launch the application. The program opens a dedicated browser window. This specific window keeps your financial tools separated from your regular web browsing for better security.

## 🛠 Using the features

### Tax calculators
The tax tool helps you estimate annual income taxes. Input your gross income and select your filing status. The tool adjusts your results based on current tax codes.

### Payroll processing
The payroll calculator assists small business owners. Enter hours worked and pay rates. The system determines tax withholdings and net pay for your employees.

### Service packages
Review the list of professional accounting services. This section details our standard and premium tiers. You can view the cost and list of deliverables for each package here.

### Client resources
This tab contains templates, guides, and digital forms. You can download these files to your computer for offline use. 

### Multilingual support
Apex MMC supports several languages to assist users in their preferred tongue. Click the settings icon in the top right corner to change your language display settings. The application saves this choice for future sessions.

## 🛡 Security and privacy

Your financial data stays on your local machine during most operations. We do not store your private tax figures on our servers unless you choose to sync your account for team collaboration. Always keep your local Windows login password secure to protect your financial files.

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

**The app does not open:** Check your task manager to see if the process is already running. End the task and try to open it again.

**The app looks blurry:** Right-click the desktop icon, select Properties, click Compatibility, and check "Disable fullscreen optimizations."

**The connection fails:** Ensure your firewall allows Apex MMC to access the internet. You can check this in your Windows Security settings under "Allow an app through firewall."

**Updates:** The application checks for updates every time you open it. If a new version exists, a prompt will appear. Click "Install update" to keep your tools current.

## 📂 Managing your files

Apex MMC saves your documents in your "Documents" folder under "ApexMMCData." You should back up this folder regularly to an external drive or a cloud storage service like OneDrive or Google Drive. This protects your records if your computer hardware fails.
